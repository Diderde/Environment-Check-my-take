# Environment Check (my take)

> 原件存档 / Original archive: [`Original File.py`](./Original%20File.py)

这是我的早期作品之一，那时候我还是一个甚至会在全局环境里进行开发的傻子。

当时我常常抱怨自己的环境问题，所以有了这个，毫无疑问，这是一坨彻彻底底的狗屎。

所以，我觉得有必要把这个原件公开，希望作为一个反面教材来提供学习。

之后我会考虑重写，不过那是后话了。

---

## English

This is one of my early works, from a time when I was still a fool who would do development right inside the global environment.

I used to complain constantly about my environment troubles, and that is how this came to be. Without a doubt, it is a complete and utter piece of shit.

That is exactly why I feel the need to make the original public — in the hope that it may serve as a cautionary tale for learning.

A rewrite is under consideration, but that is a story for another day.
